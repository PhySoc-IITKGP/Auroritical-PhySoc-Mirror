What We Do

We host a variety of events to help bring the physics community at IITKGP closer! Members can pursue their interests in physics in rigorous competitions or more laidback socials; PhySoc is whatever you make of it. We are also here to help support you, so please feel free to talk to other members about how to make the most of your physics experience at KGP!
