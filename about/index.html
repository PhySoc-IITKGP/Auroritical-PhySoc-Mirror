About Physics Society

The Physics Society (PhySoc) at IIT Kharagpur is the official student body representing undergraduate, postgraduate, and research scholars in the Department of Physics. We facilitate academic mentorship, scientific seminars, research discussions, and community social events.

PhySoc Academic Wing

Department of Physics, IIT Kharagpur

The academic core of PhySoc — facilitating peer mentorship, course roadmaps, subject handbooks, research seminars, and specialized interest groups across theoretical & experimental physics domains.
