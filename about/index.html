About Physics Society (PhySoc)

What We Do

We host a variety of events to help bring the physics community at IIT Kharagpur closer! Members can pursue their interests in physics through rigorous competitions, observation nights, or laidback socials. PhySoc is whatever you make of it. We are also here to support you in your academic journey!
