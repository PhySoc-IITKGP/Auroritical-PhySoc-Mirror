Welcome to PhySoc!

We are the student-run Physics Society of IIT Kharagpur, based out of the Department of Physics. We represent a community of students who are passionate about physics, ranging from undergraduate Physics majors to postgraduate scholars and research students across other engineering disciplines.

Selections & Membership

PhySoc is a selective student working group. We recruit active members annually via official department selection rounds. General membership is not open without selection.

  • Executive Selections: Selections are conducted at the start of semesters. Check the Announcements section for selection dates.
  • Profile Selections: Candidates are interviewed for specific positions: resource management, event coordination, web development, and digital outreach.
  • Follow Selection News: All recruitment briefs are posted on our official Instagram and LinkedIn pages.

About Physics Society, IIT Kharagpur

Founded to bridge the gap between classroom theory and cutting-edge physics research, PhySoc is the student forum of the Department of Physics at IIT Kharagpur. We aim to inspire curiosity, foster high-quality research practices, and create a strong community of thinkers.

Our Mission

Promoting physics literacy, organizing academic workshops, telescope observation nights, and research panel discussions.

Research Focus

Connecting students with research projects, providing peer mentorship, and guiding students on external internships.

Community

Hosting interactive tea-table physics discussions, movie screenings, quizzes, and annual symposiums.

Department of Physics, IIT Kharagpur

The Department of Physics at IIT Kharagpur is one of the oldest and most prestigious departments in the country, starting its journey in 1951. Over the decades, it has evolved into a premier hub for education and research. The department hosts advanced laboratory facilities, runs the Integrated M.Sc. (5-Year), Joint M.Sc.-Ph.D. program, and Ph.D. researches across diverse fields including astrophysics, condensed matter physics, quantum computation, high-energy particle physics, and nonlinear optics.

Welcome to Course PH!

Entering the world of physics can be both exhilarating and overwhelming. Here is a curated checklist of survival tips, textbooks, and software skills you should pick up early.

Recommended Textbooks

  • Classical Mechanics: An Introduction to Mechanics by Kleppner & Kolenkow, and Classical Mechanics by Herbert Goldstein.
  • Electrodynamics: Introduction to Electrodynamics by David J. Griffiths.
  • Quantum Mechanics: Introduction to Quantum Mechanics by David J. Griffiths, or Quantum Physics by Stephen Gasiorowicz.

Technical Software Skills

Modern physics relies heavily on computational modeling and typesetting. Start learning these as early as possible:

  • Python (NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib)
  • LaTeX (Academic document typesetting)
  • Mathematica / Jupyter Notebooks
  • Linux Command Line & Bash

Finding a Research Project (UROP Guide)

At IIT Kharagpur, getting hands-on research experience under professors is highly encouraged. This is our equivalent of MIT's Undergraduate Research Opportunities Program (UROP).

1
Identify Your Area

Read through the research profiles of professors on the Department Webpage. Focus on what excites you: condensed matter, gravitation, quantum computing, or nuclear physics.

2
Do Your Homework

Before emailing a professor, read one of their recent publications or review articles in their field. Understand the basic concepts they work on.

3
Write a Professional Email

Keep it concise. State your academic background, your interest in their specific field, and mention a paper of theirs you looked at. Ask for a brief 10-minute meeting.

Recommended Email Template 
Subject: Inquiry regarding research projects under your guidance - [Your Name]

Dear Professor [Professor's Last Name],

I am a [Year] student pursuing [Your Degree] in Physics (or your branch). I have been reading about your work on [Research Topic/Title] and found your recent paper on [Brief description of their paper/work] highly intriguing.

I am very interested in working on a research project in this domain under your guidance. I have prior coursework in [Mention relevant subjects, e.g., Quantum Mechanics / Python Programming] which I believe would be useful.

Would you be available for a brief meeting sometime this week to discuss potential opportunities in your lab? I have attached my CV for your reference.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

Summer Research Opportunities

Securing a summer fellowship is a great way to experience research at other premier institutions globally and within India. Here are some of the most sought-after fellowships by physics students:

IAS-SRFP
National

Indian Academy of Sciences Summer Research Fellowship Program. Placements at top institutes like IISc, TIFR, HRI, and IITs.

Timeline: October - November
DAAD WISE
International

Research internships at German public universities and institutes. Highly competitive, covering travel and living costs.

Timeline: September - October
Mitacs Globalink
International

Fully-funded summer internships at universities in Canada. Excellent cross-cultural and academic experience.

Timeline: July - August
S.N. Bose Scholars
International

A prestigious student exchange program for research at partner universities in the United States.

Timeline: September - October
