UROP Application Guide

The Undergraduate Research Opportunities Programme (UROP) is your gateway to department research. Follow this roadmap to secure a project.

1
Identify Research Areas

Review the faculty profiles on the Physics Dept Site. Look for recent publications.

2
Read Their Papers

Don't cold email blindly. Skim their last 2-3 papers. You don't need to understand the math, just the goal.

3
Send the Email

Keep it concise. State your year, course, interest in their specific paper, and attach your CV.

Email Template

Dear Prof. [Name],

I am a [Year] year student pursuing [Course] in the Physics Department. I am writing to express my interest in joining your research group for a UROP project this semester.

I recently read your paper on "[Paper Title]" and was particularly fascinated by [1 specific thing about the paper]. I have background knowledge in [Relevant Subject/Skill, e.g., Statistical Mechanics, Python] and would love to contribute to your ongoing work in this area.

I have attached my CV and transcript for your reference. Would it be possible to schedule a brief meeting this week to discuss potential projects?

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]
